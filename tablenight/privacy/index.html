Table Night: Score Keeper — Privacy policy

Summary

Table Night keeps score for your game nights. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no internet access.

No account, no analytics, no ads

You do not create an account or sign in. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting software.

No internet access

Table Night requests one Android permission, com.android.vending.BILLING, which Google Play needs to sell the optional Table Night Unlock. It does not request internet access.

Your data stays on your phone

Player names, scores and photos stay on the device, and in Android backup if you have it turned on.

Purchases

Google Play processes purchases. Refunds go through your Google Play order history: https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics leave the phone only when you send them yourself, from your own mail app or share target. Table Night transmits nothing on its own.

Deleting your data

Delete your data inside the app, or uninstall Table Night to remove everything it stored on the device.

Support

Support is by email only: support@insight-flowai.com
